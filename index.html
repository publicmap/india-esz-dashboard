QA: protected areas not yet matched to Wikidata (–)

These protected area names and states come from MoEF ESZ notifications but couldn't be automatically matched to a Wikidata item — usually a spelling variant, a notification covering multiple parks, or an item genuinely missing from Wikidata. To fix one, add a row to data/corrections.csv with columns paName, state, correctPaName, correctState, correctPaType — the paName/state as they appear below, and the corrected values to re-run the join with — then open a pull request against the repo.

Protected areas without a Wikidata coordinate (–)
